Kuroush Allameh

I am a PhD student in theoretical physics at the University of California, Santa Cruz, advised by Edgar Shaghoulian. Before coming to Santa Cruz, I did my undergraduate studies at Sharif University of Technology.

I work on quantum gravity, holography, and conformal field theory. Lately I have been thinking about AdS3 gravity at finite cutoff, timelike Liouville theory, TT deformations, and flat-space limits of holography.
